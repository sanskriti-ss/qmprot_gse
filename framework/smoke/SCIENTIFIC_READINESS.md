## What a benchmark row measures

Every completed fit stays in its configured cohort, including fits with the wrong electron number. The saved state gives the full active-electron count distribution, the probability outside the molecule's target count, mean spin projection, probability of the target spin projection, and mean total spin squared. A superposition can give a fractional probability. For contextual calculations these observables refer to the original spin orbitals after restoring the stabilizer eigenvalues and undoing the rotations. The noisy score uses the output density matrix. The ideal circuit at the same angles has its own score. CB uses the selected two-state Ritz vector. [Endpoint calculations](../experiments/endpoint_sector.py) · [Independent controls](test_endpoint_sector.py)

On frozen full-H₂ prefix fits across eleven methods, two requested optimizers and paired seeds 42 and 43, **12 of 44** endpoints had more than 1% probability of the wrong electron count. Six exceeded 50%. All 44 remained in the results and every energy replayed within 6.7 × 10⁻¹⁶ Ha. On contextual glycine the independently lifted 12-qubit parent operator reproduced the eight-qubit model within 3.41 × 10⁻¹³ Ha. The model's lowest possible energy lies **24.772 mHa above parent CASCI**, so the usual 1.6 mHa threshold is unreachable on that operator. A fresh 10-parameter, 100-evaluation native HE fit on this projection had 49.53% probability outside the intended six-electron sector. Trainability saves this projection floor separately from optimizer error and stores the final angles, active generators and input digest. ## Paired fermionic ADAPT comparison

The registered `adapt_vqe` uses approximate RY/CNOT excitations. Its double can move a state even at zero angle. A central difference about that point can therefore measure a derivative around a different state from the one being extended. Both registered approximate runs stopped at the first gradient screen with the HF determinant. Genuine Jordan–Wigner rotations recovered the missing correlation energy. The approximate circuit and the genuine circuit were also run with identical pools to isolate the gate implementation.

| Molecule and unprojected active space | Registered approximate error above CASCI | Approximate, matched pool | Genuine fermionic, matched pool |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| H₂, two electrons in two orbitals | 19.340691 mHa, 26 calls | 19.340691 mHa, 8 calls | 8.44 × 10⁻¹⁵ Ha, 55 calls |
| Glycine, two electrons in canonical HOMO/LUMO | 0.045288 mHa, 26 calls | 0.045288 mHa, 8 calls | 2.56 × 10⁻¹² Ha, 190 calls |

Each fit used the same initial determinant, seed, COBYLA tolerances, cap of 300 molecular energy evaluations and at most three additions. The native pool has 12 candidates, the matched pool three. Genuine ADAPT accepted one excitation on H₂ and three on glycine. With the same double at 0.4 radians on H₂ the approximate gate produced 98.03% probability of the wrong electron count. Genuine rotations matched independent dense fermionic exponentials. The two approximate optimization endpoints had zero charge leakage because they accepted no gates. The paired comparison also ran on Modal. The final Modal genuine-gate residuals were 5.55 × 10⁻¹⁵ Ha on H₂ and 2.05 × 10⁻¹² Ha on glycine, at 59 and 202 evaluations respectively. The full companion comparison took 2.35 s locally and 7.81 s on Modal. [Paired on-host measurements](reproductions/study_checks/paired_hosts.json) These tests establish the mechanism and its size on the stated active spaces. There is no measured upper bound on the error for larger molecules. November trainability uses the distinct Qubit-ADAPT implementation. | Initialization and barren plateaus | Reconstruct finite-difference gradients and the per-parameter variance over independent draws. Check actual seeded angles and the objective at each start. A near-zero HE rotation can still start far from molecular HF because of fixed CNOTs. The batch analysis pools reference-relative errors across molecules. Four-qubit gradient statistics cannot establish exponential scaling with qubit count. |
| Noise resilience | Independently propagate density matrices for six supported fixed circuits. On 42 hydrogen baseline, zero-strength and noisy rows, recorded energy and parameter drift agreed within 5 × 10⁻¹¹ at CSV precision. Noise studies apply independent single-qubit channels at configured circuit locations and optimize under those channels, then rescore on the clean Hamiltonian. They represent sensitivity to specified channels. Gate-dependent calibration, crosstalk, readout error and finite-shot variation are outside that comparison. |
| Trainability and cost | Record a crossing of molecular HF separately from attaining a specified absolute error to CASCI. Include censored runs, callback evaluations and partially exhausted screens. ## Preparation and historical results

The original true-iQCC update added a real multiple of a commutator of Hermitian operators to a Hermitian Hamiltonian. That term is anti-Hermitian. A unitary basis change must preserve the eigenvalues. Complex-to-real casting in the simulation obscured the violation. Four historical revisions gave the same glycine answer 11.093333 mHa below the lowest eigenvalue of their own 26-term operator. Exact Pauli rotations recovered that eigenvalue within 1.7 × 10⁻¹³ Ha. The historical Figure 12 ends at ten terms. Its original ordered inputs and per-case data are outstanding. [Historical reproduction](reproductions/iqcc_glycine/README.md)

The contextual projection originally rotated the reference ket with the opposite sign to the Hamiltonian. A three-qubit control gave orthogonal reference states. Two fresh glycine preparations verified that the audited 27-term Hamiltonian and its normalized reference matched the frozen fixture. The implementation saves its rotation sequence, sector and retained HF probability. Correcting sulfur metadata changed neither the pinned cysteine nor methionine active-orbital ranks. Glutamine and glutamic acid have distinct input identifiers and coordinates. [Chemistry audit](chemistry_audit.md)

H₂ and all 19 pinned amino acids were checked in both the canonical and MP2 natural orbital interpretations. All 40 mapped, fixed-electron and spin-projection ground energies matched the corresponding PySCF CASCI calculation within 1.71 × 10⁻¹² Ha. On the 19 amino acids the natural-orbital CASCI energy was 45.79–88.64 mHa lower at the same selected ranks. The glycine comparison also covers 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 active qubits. Production currently prepares canonical orbitals. The scientific choice remains open. Open-shell preparation and the published asparagine spin assignment remain outside the checked set. [Orbital and qubit measurements](reproductions/study_checks/orbital_comparison.json) · [Pinned geometries](geometries)

Fresh glycine preparations also exposed a reproducibility issue for prefix studies. The local and Modal runs from identical scientific source each produced 27 terms and retained essentially all of HF. They selected different contextual stabilizers. At ten terms, the exact minimum moved from −279.155840647 Ha to −279.168622255 Ha, a 12.782 mHa difference. All three checked 27-term minimum energies agreed within 3 × 10⁻¹³ Ha. Aligning the two cloud encodings by a wire permutation and two controlled-Z gates showed that only six of the first ten Pauli terms matched in a common basis. True iQCC accepted three generators locally and zero on this Modal prefix. The search is time-limited and compares floating-point candidate weights. The archived data establish different selected stabilizers and cannot isolate search timing from close candidate weights. The prepared Hamiltonian, term order and ket must travel together for a paired solver comparison.
