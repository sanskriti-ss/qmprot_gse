## Numerical distinction in the prefix results

In the saved four-qubit glycine HE case at ten terms and seed 42, the optimized prefix energy was −278.955937423801 Ha. The unrestricted ten-term ground energy was −279.155840647255 Ha and parent CASCI was −279.137662914624 Ha. Reconstructing that exact fitted state from its angles and contracting the **complete 27-term contextual operator** gave −278.789134937895 Ha. The reported distance to full CASCI was 0.181725490824 Ha. Scoring the same state on all 27 terms gives a distance of 0.348527976729 Ha. A study of state quality under truncation should use the latter quantity. These data came from the local five-study acceptance, with ordered input SHA-256 `5d1da4e2be2af4ccf918c9ade7fc08adc129ce80c253cfac27c995aa350b1539`. The 16 amplitudes and both expectations were checked by explicit dense Pauli Kronecker products. ## Active space and sector

The campaign takes ranks of MP2 natural occupation eigenvalues and uses the selected integer positions in the canonical HF orbital matrix for CASCI. Those positions also index the alternative natural orbital matrix in the matched comparison. The unreduced glycine input in this review recorded STO-3G, ranks 17–22, six active electrons, 12 qubits and 1,819 ordered terms. The canonical CASCI was −279.137662914624 Ha and molecular HF was −279.111514728338 Ha. All 40 fixed-sector mapped-operator checks across H₂ and 19 amino acids agreed with the corresponding PySCF CASCI within 1.71 × 10⁻¹² Ha. Natural orbitals lowered CASCI by 45.79–88.64 mHa for the 19 amino acids. The benchmark orbital choice remains open. A matched solver comparison requires the same ordered operator and initial ket, and physical projected-sector diagnostics also require its saved parent transform. [Orbital comparison](reproductions/study_checks/orbital_comparison.json) · [Preparation and projection audit](chemistry_audit.md)

Two limitations affect comparisons with the paper. The original ordered prefix inputs are unavailable, and fresh time-limited contextual searches can choose different stabilizers on different hosts. The registered approximate fermionic ADAPT also has a double excitation which changes its starting state at zero angle. Its paired genuine Jordan–Wigner control and the separate Qubit-ADAPT algorithm are explicitly identified in the records. ## First Qubit-ADAPT screen

A complete single-thread Lightning screen on the frozen 12-qubit glycine input scored every member of the 972-generator pool at both signs of 10⁻⁵. All **1,944 native statevectors had distinct bit patterns** and three populated coordinates. For the selected generator `Y2 X3 X6 X7` at positive displacement, the third amplitude was 1.7968282226758285 × 10⁻²¹ i. Dropping it would change the computed state. A full-screen exact-state cache made 1,944 native measurements, just as the stock cache did. A separately repeated reference screen for a different target on the exact same model can reuse the previously measured displacements. [Production probe controls](test_trainability_endpoints.py)

The paired probes *can* share their gate decomposition. On the inspected wheel the negative partner has the same ordered native gates and static angles as the positive partner. The sign of one MultiRZ angle changes. A local prototype rebound that one gate and retained state reset, Lightning gate execution, support filtering, native measurement and evaluation order. The complete statevector traces, all energies and the final gradient vectors agreed bit for bit in a control, candidate, candidate, control sequence. The largest gradient was 0.2718742109664163 at pool index 612. Mean setup plus first screen fell from 10.48 s to 6.08 s, **1.72×**. If the same 4.40 s were saved once in the measured 46.43 s six-fit glycine trainability grid, its projected total would be 42.03 s, **1.10×**. This extrapolation relies on the existing cross-target probe reuse and excludes any gain after the reference screen. A production implementation still needs checked decomposition, device, tracker and call-order guards and a paired Modal test.
