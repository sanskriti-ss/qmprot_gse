# Molecular preparation and contextual projection audit

## Inputs and frozen cores

The pinned 19-amino-acid set has 19 distinct input IDs. PySCF accepted each geometry’s charge (0), spin (0) and electron parity. Glutamine and glutamic acid have distinct coordinates and names, although both have 78 electrons. The published asparagine file is outside this set. Its 70 electrons and spin 1 are incompatible with PySCF’s electron-count convention. The documented scope is the 19 inputs used by the campaign. [Geometry loader](../active_space_truncation/geometry.py#L72) · [Project journal](../../JOURNAL.md)

The pinned cysteine and methionine geometries contain sulfur and have 64 and 80 electrons respectively. An incomplete atomic-number table reported 48 and 64 in the preparation log. PySCF independently constructs its molecule from the symbols, so its electronic calculations had the correct totals. The loader now uses PySCF’s element charges. Omitting sulfur’s ten frozen-core electrons also left five inner-shell orbitals eligible for active-space ranking. Counting them gives 11 frozen core orbitals in cysteine and 13 in methionine. The six canonical indices selected on the pinned STO-3G inputs were unchanged, 30–35 and 38–43 respectively, with zero-based indexing. ## Operator and reference in the same subspace

For rotations represented by a unitary U and an isometry P that inserts the fixed stabilizer bits, the reduced operator is P† U H U† P. Its reference must be the normalized P† U |HF⟩. A dense three-qubit check agreed with the returned two-qubit operator to 6.7 × 10⁻¹⁶ Ha. The ket rotation had the opposite sign from the operator rotation. The resulting normalized state had fidelity below 3 × 10⁻³² with the independently projected HF determinant, despite a correct sector probability of 0.5. The corrected ket has unit fidelity. This example exercises both the continuous-angle and Clifford rotations. [Operator rotation](../contextual_subspace/core.py#L449) · [Reference rotation](../contextual_subspace/cs_reduction.py#L121) · [Dense unitary and projector regression](test_chemistry_audit.py#L30)

For pinned H₂, direct projections from four qubits to one, two and three reproduced the reduced operators to 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁶ Ha. Each selected sector retained 0.988545 of the HF probability. The normalized initial state coincided up to phase even with the opposite sign in this particular input. For two qubits the largest absolute element of the commutator between the full electron-number operator and the selected-sector projector was 0.426. The starting state retained two electrons within roundoff. A general variational state in this subspace has no such guarantee. The reducer deliberately clears the spin-orbital count and active-electron count in its reduced Hamiltonian. [Reduced molecule metadata](../contextual_subspace/cs_reduction.py#L329)

On pinned glycine, independent reconstruction from the 1,819-term, 12-qubit Hamiltonian gave the four-qubit, 27-term operator within 4.0 × 10⁻¹³ Ha. Two fresh, 30-second-search preparations produced all 27 ordered Pauli strings and coefficients exactly as stored in the frozen input for the 26-term iQCC replay. Their normalized reference also matched the stored vector up to phase. The consistent projection retained 0.99999999999998 of HF probability. Applying the opposite sign in that same sector retained only 2.04 × 10⁻¹⁴, which happened to normalize to the same vector. The ket correction therefore changes the selection criterion on fresh preparations even where the two normalized outputs coincide. The frozen replay reads its saved input directly. Its SHA-256 is `6de2adcfdd7926b5406ab1730e26b776489757b076fe837bbf5fce54a0cfa042`. [Archived input](reproductions/iqcc_glycine/glycine.json) · [Replay](reproductions/iqcc_glycine/replay.py)

## Remaining boundaries

- The low-level preparation pipeline records the SCF and CASCI convergence flags and proceeds to assemble the qubit Hamiltonian. The production experiment loader checks both flags as soon as preparation returns. It raises a `ValueError` before contextual reduction or solver execution if either failed. The independent orbital comparison also checks convergence. [Preparation](../active_space_truncation/run_pipeline.py#L68) · [Experiment loader](../experiments/_common.py#L136) · [Independent checks](active_spaces.py#L45)
- The noncontextual search has a wall-time cutoff. The continuous optimization for more than two anticommuting cliques uses differential evolution without an explicit seed. The reducer can relax an unattainable target or return the original Hamiltonian after a search failure. If no tested sector has a numerically resolvable HF projection, it now raises an error. The H₂ and glycine runs above produced the requested sizes and normalized vectors. The saved CS metadata now includes the ordered Pauli rotation generators and their angles in radians for exp(+iθG/2), fixed rotated stabilizers and eigenvalues, the selected noncontextual energy, and the squared norm of the projected HF state before normalization. A reduced-input snapshot can replay the downstream model independently of search timing. [Search](../contextual_subspace/core.py#L346) · [Target handling](../contextual_subspace/cs_reduction.py#L248) · [Sector fallback](../contextual_subspace/cs_reduction.py#L277) · [Reduction metadata](../contextual_subspace/cs_reduction.py#L352)
- The two-qubit operator 0.2 XI + 0.3 ZI can be reduced with zero commuting generators. Its one-qubit projection is the scalar −√0.13 times identity. A regression checks both this case and a unit identity offset. A scalar-only input returns its original operator and reports that no noncontextual stabilizers were found. [Independent generator construction](../contextual_subspace/core.py#L113)
- The 19 accepted inputs are closed-shell geometries. This audit does not establish the preparation of open-shell spin states, VQE particle-sector conservation, or that the chosen active orbitals recover full-molecule energies. Full active-space CASCI and the ground energy of a restricted CS Hamiltonian answer different questions. The experiment loader retains the former in `casci_energy`. A comparison to the ground energy of the actual projected operator requires its independent diagonalization. [Experiment references](../experiments/_common.py#L137)
