## The paper's noise workload

Figure 8 and section 2.5 specify six qubits, two layers and hardware-efficient VQE. Figure 9 shows **16 molecules**, three channels and five positive strengths plus zero. For one seed, that is **240 positive fits and 16 independent clean fits**. The code copies each clean fit to the three zero-noise rows. Figure 8 shows the slice at 0.02, and Figure 17 shows cosine similarity for the same conditions. HVA appears in the separate Hamiltonian-prefix comparison through ten terms. The saved plotting script names the May 9 six-qubit HE batch. [Manuscript, pp. 7, 12–15, 23](../../QMProt.pdf) · [Figure input path](../../prof_figures/paper_combined_figure.py#L31)

The caption calls the circuit RY and gives 36 angles. The May 9 solver defaults to RY plus RZ, which gives 36 angles at six qubits and two layers. RY alone gives 18. The likely historical circuit starts in the zero state. The exact original input and per-fit run config are unavailable. [May 9 ansatz](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/2264f87/framework/algorithms/vqe_hardware_efficient.py#L27-L71)

A reconstruction on genuine glycine projected 12 qubits to six, with 118 terms. Across the three channels at strength 0.01, the May code averaged 1.111 s per warmed energy. The optimized local code averaged 0.0590 s at identical angles and settings. All nine tested energies matched bit for bit on this host. At 150 distinct calls per positive fit, this gives about 11.1 hours and 35 minutes of local objective time respectively across 16 glycine-equivalent molecules. Historical callbacks recomputed energies. Preparation, optimizer work and actual differences between molecules also contribute to whole-run elapsed time.

The other paper figures require different runs. Initialization used 24 molecules or fragments, three starts and five Bayesian trials per start (360 clean HE fits). The paper's adaptive accuracy figure stopped at three additions. Its matched-parameter trainability figure used HE and Qubit-ADAPT at 10, 50 and 100 requested parameters, with many plotted adaptive counts above 10,000. The November cap changes this comparison. The June-dated figure input identifies the source for a historical plot. It supplies no timing evidence for the recent week estimate.
