## How the price was calculated

- **May noise.** The historical solver at `2264f87` took 3.47–4.11 s per warm energy on Modal, averaged separately by channel on a reconstructed 118-term glycine operator. All nine tested energy values matched the earlier Mac run bit for bit. At 150 primary evaluations per positive fit, the group costs $4.77 before callback evaluations. The historical code reevaluates the energy in a callback. Adding 86 or 150 such calls per fit gives $7.51 or $9.54. Callback counts and final stopping points in the original noise run are unknown.
- **May adaptive size, prefixes and COBYLA.** Figure 10 shows roughly 2,850 cumulative evaluations at three operators. At one second per historical evaluation, 16–20 contributing molecules would cost $1.60–$2.00. Prefix calibration uses 110 measured fits through ten terms on four-qubit glycine and gives about $0.11–$0.13 for 20–24 comparable inputs. The original ordered operators and fitting budgets for Figure 12 are missing. A separate six-qubit COBYLA initialization pilot took 0.87 local seconds at 200 requested calls, with 59 callbacks. At the historical batch defaults and 16 plotted molecules it gives a rough $0.03–$0.06, including preparation.
- **June trainability.** Digitizing Figure 16 gives approximately 579,700 Qubit-ADAPT evaluations and 9,067 HE evaluations. The June solver at `49f1c5c` took about one second per sampled energy on Modal with 1, 10, 50 and 100 generators, on a real 12-qubit, 1,819-term operator. Assigning target-depth glycine timings to the plotted adaptive counts, plus a small HE allowance, gives $20.40. The 19 amino acids account for roughly 519,300 adaptive calls. The other five systems, actual generator trajectories and active spaces add uncertainty. The November 2,000-call cap would truncate many of the plotted fits.
- **June initialization.** A 136-parameter, 16-layer four-qubit historical Bayesian fit used 200 evaluations and took 50.74 s on this Mac. For 360 fits that is 5.07 local hours. Multiplying by the 3.49 cloud/local ratio from a separate four-qubit forest fit gives $2.24. A 2–4× host sensitivity gives $1.28–$2.56. Historical gradient sampling took 1.35 local seconds for 900 shifted energies on one prepared molecule. Chemistry and plots add some overhead. [Paired host calibration](DEEP_EFFICIENCY.md)

The Modal calibration processed 24 adaptive and nine noisy energies in 71 worker-seconds. **What can be reproduced.** The estimate covers every numbered figure and table in the final manuscript, including its appendices, with each underlying fit counted once. Bit-for-bit reconstruction from the checkout is currently impossible for several figures: some original ordered Hamiltonians, per-fit configurations, seeds and optimizer outputs are missing. The noise caption also combines a pure-RY description with 36 parameters, consistent with the contemporary RY/RZ default. The timing uses that 36-angle circuit and the pinned scientific environment available today. Obtaining the missing author artifacts could change the budget and is necessary to certify exact numbers.
