# Methionine in the October manuscript

Figure 4 on page 9 of `QMProt.pdf` shows a 16-layer methionine hardware-efficient VQE study with five trials per initialization. The three means fluctuate near −787.1 Ha, about 0.92 Ha above the two reference lines. The legend gives molecular HF as −788.02139 Ha and active-space CASCI as −788.02271 Ha. The manuscript describes this run as noiseless. An independently saved November calculation agrees with both reference values when rounded to the published precision. [October manuscript](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/4a23de80b29e4778f93fc754b198e3ffafb6b245/QMProt.pdf)

## What the figure measures

At the manuscript commit, the Bayesian objective appended **every evaluated energy** to the history. The per-molecule writer took the mean and population standard deviation across available trials at each acquisition index. The returned fit used the optimizer’s minimum. A flat mean of successive proposals does not establish that its incumbent stopped improving on acquisition 2 or 3. Figure 5's plotted ordinate is normalized progress aggregated across 24 molecules. Figure 6's actual axis is mean final energy above the best strategy within each molecule. For methionine, relative to small-random initialization, the raster gives about **4.3 mHa** for near-identity and **13.2 mHa** for random-uniform. The plot does not supply the absolute endpoint means or a significance test. [October convergence writer](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/4a23de80b29e4778f93fc754b198e3ffafb6b245/framework/experiments/barren_plateau.py#L500-L535) · [Bayesian objective](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/4a23de80b29e4778f93fc754b198e3ffafb6b245/framework/core/base_vqe.py#L415-L434)

The shading gives a conditional lower bound on any recorded acquisition. For five values of mean m and population standard deviation s, the minimum is at least m−2s. This follows from the sum of the four other deviations and Cauchy–Schwarz. With shaded edges L=m−s and U=m+s, the bound is (3L−U)/2. Taking the lowest L and highest U across *all* strategies at each horizontal position only loosens the bound. The native 1,200 × 750 raster, widened by two horizontal pixels and ten vertical pixels per edge, gives **approximately −787.83 Ha** as a conservative lower bound. If the archived ±1 population-SD writer produced the figure as its labels indicate, even the most favorable evaluation represented in it is about **0.19 Ha or more above HF**. This uses the ink on the page; it does not recover individual minima. Recheck using Python with NumPy, Pillow and pypdf. [Raster measurement](methionine_figure.py)

## What can be attributed

The October driver defaults to seed 42, five paired seeds per strategy, 200 Bayesian calls and a four-qubit CS target. The streams start from uniform angles in [0,2π], [−π/8,π/8] and [−0.05,0.05]. The HE source in the manuscript commit used RY/RZ walls and CNOTs starting in the simulator's zero state. It did not load the projected HF ket. At four qubits and depth 16 this would give 136 angles, a forest surrogate and 46 initial samples. Random-uniform starts could expand the Bayesian search box beyond [−π,π]. Small-random and near-identity shared the same search box and trial seed. Their traces overlay through the early random proposals, consistent with these defaults. The first optional HE HF preparation landed in the manuscript commit's child about forty minutes later. The paper artifact does not establish that exact source or the defaults for the underlying run. [June driver](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/240508a/run_barren_plateau_part_b_all_molecules.py) · [October HE](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/4a23de80b29e4778f93fc754b198e3ffafb6b245/framework/algorithms/vqe_hardware_efficient.py#L63-L109) · [Search bounds and backend](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/blob/4a23de80b29e4778f93fc754b198e3ffafb6b245/framework/core/base_vqe.py#L349-L395) · [Subsequent HF option](https://github.com/sanskriti-ss/qmprot_gse/commit/a7a6e03)

## Independent methionine cross-check

The November 19-amino-acid trainability batch used genuine methionine, STO-3G and 12 unprojected qubits. Its HF and CASCI were **−788.0213861360659** and **−788.0227139834731 Ha**, a gap of **1.3278474 mHa**. The small-random RY hardware-efficient runs included HF preparation and used seed 42 and COBYLA. Each HE or Qubit-ADAPT case had a budget of 2,000 molecular energy evaluations. HE passed 2,000 to COBYLA as its limit. The 500-iteration ceiling applied to individual ADAPT inner fits. The HE rows at 50 and 100 parameters used 2,000 calls and recorded 1,341 and 1,192 outer iterations, respectively.
